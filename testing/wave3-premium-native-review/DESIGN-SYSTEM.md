# Adjuster Network Premium Native Design System

## Character

The visual system combines a professional field publication, a private member
network, and an operations desk. Information and member identity lead;
decoration supports hierarchy rather than competing with it.

## Typography

- Display: compact, high-authority page identity with slightly tightened tracking.
- Page and section titles: strong but materially smaller than display type.
- Topic titles: readable editorial weight with room for two-line scanning.
- Body: 15–16 point base with 22–24 point line height for sustained reading.
- Metadata: compact, tabular where numeric, and subordinate to authorship.
- Eyebrows: sparse uppercase labels with increased tracking for desk/category context.

## Spacing and density

- An 4/6/10/16/24/32/48 rhythm governs layout.
- Lists use dividers and whitespace rather than card containers.
- Featured information receives one intentional surface; supporting rows remain flat.
- Touch targets remain at least 44 points even when visual density is tighter.

## Surfaces and depth

- Canvas is a warm neutral in light mode and near-black blue in dark mode.
- Standard, alternate, raised, and warm editorial surfaces have explicit roles.
- Featured surfaces use a restrained left rule instead of a heavy outline.
- Shadows are reserved for anchored or genuinely elevated controls.

## Color

- AN navy anchors authority and briefing surfaces.
- AN red identifies brand, active navigation, and featured editorial structure.
- Blue/cyan supports links and information actions rather than decorating everything.
- Semantic success, warning, and destructive colors retain their existing meanings.

## Components

- Primary headers integrate the compact AN mark, page title, context, and a red rule.
- Nested headers preserve the shared native back treatment and compact brand mark.
- Topic rows emphasize avatar, author/context, title, and activity metadata.
- Pills are compact filters, with high-contrast selected state and no inflated chrome.
- Utility rows use dividers and selected surfaces rather than independent cards.
- Empty/loading/error states retain the certified truthful behavior and use structured,
  calm presentation.

## Accessibility

Dynamic Type, VoiceOver semantics, reduced-motion posture, keyboard avoidance,
safe areas, and 44-point interaction targets remain design constraints rather
than exceptions.
